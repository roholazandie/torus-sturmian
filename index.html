Factor Complexity

p(n) counts the distinct factors of length n. Sturmian words are exactly the aperiodic words with p(n) = n + 1; a periodic word of period L stops at L.

observed p(n) n + 1

Balance

Number of 1s in a window of length n, over every window seen so far (min–max). In a balanced word any two windows of the same length differ by at most one 1.

Letter Frequency

D(N) = (number of 1s in the first N letters) − N/(1+α). The frequency of 1s tends to 1/(1+α), and D(N) never leaves a band of width 1: the error is bounded, not just small. N is on a log scale.

About 3D Torus Sturmian Words

The particle moves along θ₁(t) = t (around the tube) and θ₂(t) = α·t + 2πρ (around the central hole). On the unwrapped square this is a straight line of slope α; leaving through the right edge writes 1, leaving through the top edge writes 0. Crossing times are computed exactly, so no crossing is missed at any speed or slope.

When α is irrational, the path never closes and the word is Sturmian: it has exactly n + 1 distinct factors of each length n. With ρ = 0 it is the characteristic word of α. (Typed slopes are stored as floating-point numbers, so this holds up to machine precision.)

When α = p/q in lowest terms, the path closes after q turns around the tube and p turns around the hole, so the word is periodic with period p + q (q ones and p zeros). With ρ = 0 the path starts and ends at a corner of the square. There both angles wrap at once and the corner rule decides whether to write 10 or 01. The period is then a palindrome followed by that pair, a rotation of a Christoffel word.
